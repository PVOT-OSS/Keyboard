Pvot Keyboard is a light, private keyboard with glide typing, clipboard history and emoji search. It has no internet permission, so what you type never leaves your phone.

Type the way you like
• Glide typing, with suggestions and next-word predictions
• Auto-correction you can tune, and undo with backspace
• Dictionaries for 29 languages built in, with no download needed
• Multilingual typing: write in several languages without switching
• Optional number row, arrow keys, long-press hints and keyboard gestures

Clipboard and editing
• Clipboard history with pins, search, images and shortcuts that expand as you type
• Smart chips for copied links, email addresses and phone numbers
• A text editing panel to select, copy, cut, paste, undo and redo

Emoji
• Search emoji by name, offline
• Emoji suggestions while you type

Make it yours
• Themes: Pvot, Material You, light, dark, OLED and high contrast, each with a live preview
• Your own photo behind the keys
• Choose and order the toolbar keys
• One-handed, floating, split and resizable keyboard
• Key borders and key text size

Corrections that learn you
• Block words you never want suggested
• Import and export your personal dictionary
• Incognito mode for typing that should not be learnt
• Typing stats kept only on your phone

Private and open
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• No internet permission: your words, clipboard and settings stay on your phone
• Open source, based on the Android Open Source Project keyboard
